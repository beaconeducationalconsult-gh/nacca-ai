NOTES FOR THE TEACHER 
• This Scheme of Learning was auto-generated from the NaCCA Mathematics Curriculum for Primary Schools (Basic 1 –3, September 2019). 
• Each row corresponds to one CONTENT INDICATOR. Where a strand has more indicators than weeks, two indicators are grouped in t he same week; where fewer, weeks are filled 
with REVISION/CONSOLIDATION. 
• The Lower Primary Mathematics curriculum has 4 strands: 1. Number · 2. Algebra · 3. Geometry and Measurement · 4. Data — identical to Upper Primary. Each week integrates 
indicators from multiple strands. 
• Each week is planned for Monday–Friday with 10 periods of 30 minutes. Teachers should distribute the indicators across the periods at their professional disc retion. 
• At Lower Primary, emphasise HANDS-ON, CONCRETE-TO-ABSTRACT learning: use counters, multi-base blocks, real objects, role-play (shop, classroom positioning), pictures, and 
song-based number drills. 
• Number Strand 1 (especially counting and number operations) takes the LARGEST share of weekly time — plan accordingly. 
• The suggested Core Competencies, T/L Resources, Keywords and Assessment items are starting points only — adapt them to your class, school context and available materials.  
• Always pair this Scheme of Learning with the full curriculum document, the official NaCCA exemplars, and approved Maths textb ooks/manipulatives. 
• Sub-strand titles in this Scheme of Learning have been extracted directly from the explicit 'Sub -Strand X:' headings in the source PDF. 
• Annotation key: codes like B1.S.SS.CS.IND mean Grade=Basic 1, Strand=S, Sub -strand=SS, Content Standard=CS, Indicator=IND.
